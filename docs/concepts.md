# Concepts
## Court Case
Single court proceeding recorded in a historical source, identified by date, court, and source text. A court case results in exactly one ruling in this model.
## Ruling
The judicial decision resulting from a court case. It records the year, a description, and whether the case was resolved or referred, and may cite a legal source.
## Outcome Type
A categorization describing the kind of decision outcome (e.g. winner, sanction, injunction with fine, partition of land).
## Person Outcome
An outcome of a ruling as it affects a specific person (e.g. being sanctioned, declared winner). Connects rulings to individuals.
## Person
A historical individual identified in the sources, with personal attributes where known (name, patronymic, birth/death year, notes).
## Person’s Entry
A contextualized appearance of a person within a specific entry of a court case. Captures the person’s role, land-right status, and how they are described in the source.
## Role
A social or legal role attributed to a person in a specific entry (e.g. Nybyggare, Sámi). Roles are contextual, not permanent identities.
## Community
A social or administrative grouping (e.g. parish or community) to which a person belongs at the time of the court case.
## Court Case Entry
A discrete unit of information extracted from a court case, typically describing a specific land-use situation, event, or claim.
## Land Use
A categorized description of how land is used or claimed in an entry (e.g. fishing, hunting, herding), based on interpretation of the source.
## Land Right Status
A description (or binary status?) of a person’s legal (or customary?) status to the land as interpreted from the entry.
## Place
A standardized geographical place associated with an entry, linked to an external authority (national survey/placename registry).
## Season
An optional temporal qualifier indicating when the event described in an entry took place (e.g. summer, winter).
## Source
The source from which court cases are excerpted (e.g. court records, archival volumes), with identifiers and metadata.
## Legal Source
A normative legal text (law code, regulation) that a ruling cites or applies.
